How to serve in 5 minutes

  1. On toast

    Warm the jar’s contents in a pan for 2–3 minutes and spoon onto toasted baguette rubbed with garlic.

  2. With pasta

    Toss hot pasta with the warmed contents of the jar. Finish with cheese and fresh pepper.

  3. In a tartlet

    Fill ready-made shortcrust tartlets, top with cheese and bake for 5 minutes at 200°C.

Ingredients and details

Net weight

Allergens

Storage
Unopened: in a cool, dry place at room temperature. Once opened: refrigerate and eat within 3 days.
